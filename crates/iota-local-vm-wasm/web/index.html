IOTA Local VM (WASM)

The full IOTA Move VM compiled to WebAssembly. Paste a base-64 BCS TransactionData; we fetch the input objects from the chosen network and simulate locally in this tab — no signing, no submission. Optionally provide the wallet's raw signature blob(s) to verify them locally, including MoveAuthenticator signatures whose accept rule lives in Move and can't be checked cryptographically.
